# Product detail research

Checked on 31 October 2026. `src/data/product-details.json` contains original short summaries of manufacturer information for 21 identifiable catalogue products. The JSON stores the supporting URLs alongside each model so the storefront can offer a visible route to the source.

These are model specifications, not a statement about London Tech's inventory. Regional radios, physical SIM/eSIM support, installed software, storage, colours, bundled accessories, condition and battery health must be confirmed for the unit sold. No stock, price, warranty, customer review, sales count or shipping promise was derived from a manufacturer's shop page. Battery and charging numbers retain their qualification; use and compatible equipment affect results.

## Model identity and editorial decisions

- The exact iPhone model names in this register were matched to Apple's technical pages. The `used` suffix is a catalogue identifier, not manufacturer evidence of a unit's condition. No battery-health or refurbishment claim is made.
- Manufacturer storage capacities describe model choices, not currently available London Tech variants. They must not populate stock selectors without merchant confirmation.
- `galaxy-a16` is explicitly the 5G product named in this catalogue; Redmi 13C is explicitly the 4G model. Details must not be transferred to similarly named LTE/5G variants.
- The HONOR X6b specification page includes expanded/virtual RAM in some headline figures. Physical RAM was not represented as the expanded total. An inconsistent secondary display footnote on that page was excluded; the displayed 720 × 1612 panel specification was used.
- The HONOR X9b global page has limited machine-readable technical content. The same manufacturer's support comparison supplies the processor, cameras and battery facts.
- Infinix SMART 9 enrichment is intentionally limited to facts visible on the official page. No unsupported SMART 10 Plus data was substituted for it.
- The Bose QuietComfort Ultra listing does not state a generation. Only shared family features were used, with an explicit request to confirm the generation. Its original-generation manual is labelled accordingly. Numerical battery endurance and USB audio capabilities are not assumed.
- The catalogue's “Elite Wireless Controller” does not identify an exact manufacturer/generation, so Xbox Series 2 specifications were not attached to it. AirPods Max generation/connector details were likewise not assumed in this file.
- Generic category entries and models without a checked exact source retain the existing catalogue/fallback presentation. This file does not invent technical detail to make them appear complete.
- Manufacturer pages sometimes change, redirect by region or render their manuals selector through JavaScript. Support-hub links are labelled as selectors rather than falsely presented as direct PDF manuals.

## Reuse boundaries

Only short factual summaries and links are stored here. Manufacturer marketing paragraphs, customer reviews and branded graphics were not copied. This register documents technical content only; gallery-image provenance belongs to the separate gallery asset manifest.
