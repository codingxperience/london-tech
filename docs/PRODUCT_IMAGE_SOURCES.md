## Selection and verification

- Downloaded actual product views, chassis and camera details, finish lineups and selected contextual-use photographs. Repeated responsive resolutions, logos and colour-picker icons were excluded.
- Images were visually reviewed on contact sheets. Every shipped image decodes as an image, and longer dimensions are limited to 1400 pixels.
- SHA-256 checks remove repeated file contents within each additional gallery and reuse a single existing local asset for byte-identical downloads.
- Canon EOS R6 Mark II, GoPro HERO13 Black and PS5 Pro receive accurate manufacturer photos in place of generic source imagery.
- Existing store-uploaded photographs and retailer/source images remain separately available where useful. Available colour, configuration, condition and package contents still require store confirmation.

## Coverage limits

Other products keep their existing galleries; some still have only one image. Entries with ambiguous model generations—generic Galaxy tablets, Apple Watch Series, Soundcore Wireless Audio, the generic DJI Osmo Pocket listing and unbranded accessories—are not padded with images of a different model. Infinix regional public pages expose only promotional hero media, so repeated responsive versions were not added. Additional original store photographs can fill these gaps without misrepresenting the item.

Manufacturer product photographs remain the property of their respective brands. Source records are attribution and provenance, not a change of ownership or an assertion that London Tech is an authorised dealer.
