# London Tech brand system

## Identity

- Public name: **London Tech**.
- Descriptor: **London Technology Store**.
- The owner-supplied logo is the source of truth for the wordmark and navy/red palette.
- Keep the storefront calm and editorial: deep navy foundations, warm white product surfaces, measured red accents, readable type and restrained motion.

## Storefront content

- Present categories and product information for comparison without implying that catalogue entries are live inventory.
- Confirm price, stock, condition, delivery, collection, warranty and service terms with the store before purchase.
- Leave address, phone/WhatsApp, email and opening hours unpublished until the owner supplies them.
- Direct enquiries to the store-information page until a confirmed public contact channel is configured.

## Preview and operational data

- Generate the social preview from the supplied London Tech logo.
- Keep the admin page labelled as a browser-local prototype; any orders, customers, reviews, prices and stock figures in it are examples only.
- Record imported catalogue-image sources in `ASSET_SOURCES.md` and remove artwork, metadata and verification material that belongs to the previous brand.
