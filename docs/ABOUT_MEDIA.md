# London Tech About Us media

These assets come from the four videos supplied by the store owner in this conversation. No stock shop, staff or community imagery was substituted. The footage was visually reviewed across its full duration using timestamped contact sheets, with selected frames then inspected at full resolution. Audio is retained for visitors to play; no audio transcription or unsupported spoken claims have been added to the page.

## Video assets

All published MP4s use H.264, 540 × 960 pixels, 30 fps, AAC stereo audio at 80 kbps and a fast-start `moov` atom before the media payload. Full durations, original aspect ratio, embedded branding, captions and any existing letterboxing are preserved. No clips were trimmed and no branding was removed. ## Editorial evidence and limits

- The shop signage reads **“Quality and trust”** and identifies phones, repairs, smart watches, audio, laptops, gaming and accessories. The footage supports describing a physical technology shop and a range of products, without promising a particular model's current availability or condition.
- The supplied product recap visibly includes **`@londontech.ug`**, together with Facebook, Instagram and TikTok symbols. A shared printed handle does not independently verify the exact public URL or ownership of each profile; use the owner's confirmed links in site settings.
- A printed bag includes the primary number `+256 788 341 111` and a different secondary number, `+256 731 464 955`. The website should continue to use the phone numbers explicitly supplied by the owner rather than silently replacing them with historical numbers visible in footage.
- The football clips establish a visible community connection and use of London Tech shirts and banners. They do not establish a founding date, charity program, ongoing sponsorship agreement, particular tournament title, or a company retail award. Trophies in these clips must not be presented as business awards.
- No wholesale or supplier relationship, employee count, customer total, store age, revenue figure, “number one” ranking, certification or product-authenticity guarantee was established by this visual review.

Review sheets and metadata are kept under the ignored `.cache/about-video-review/` folder. Published MP4s were decoded end to end with FFmpeg and inspected for fast-start MP4 structure. No frontend dependencies were added for these assets.
